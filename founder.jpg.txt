USING YOUTUBE VIDEO THUMBNAIL AS FOUNDER IMAGE

The website is currently configured to use a YouTube video thumbnail as the founder's image.

TO GET YOUR VIDEO ID:
1. Go to your YouTube playlist: https://youtube.com/playlist?list=PLwM8qckeov89vHsSNq5hQQL32b3iDF5t9
2. Click on any video in the playlist
3. Look at the URL in your browser - it will look like: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ABC123XYZ
4. Copy the part after "v=" (e.g., "ABC123XYZ") - this is your VIDEO ID

TO UPDATE THE IMAGE IN THE WEBSITE:
1. Open index.html in a text editor
2. Find line 74 (in the Founder section)
3. Replace "VIDEO_ID_HERE" with your actual video ID (appears twice on the same line)
4. Example: Change this:
   <img src="https://img.youtube.com/vi/VIDEO_ID_HERE/maxresdefault.jpg" ...
   To this:
   <img src="https://img.youtube.com/vi/ABC123XYZ/maxresdefault.jpg" ...

THUMBNAIL QUALITY OPTIONS:
- maxresdefault.jpg = Highest quality (1280x720)
- hqdefault.jpg = High quality (480x360) - used as fallback
- mqdefault.jpg = Medium quality (320x180)
- default.jpg = Standard quality (120x90)

ALTERNATIVE: Use a Regular Image File
If you prefer to use a regular image file instead:
1. Save your image as "founder.jpg" in the root directory
2. Update line 74 in index.html to:
   <img src="founder.jpg" alt="Founder of Elite Fit Circle" class="founder-image">
